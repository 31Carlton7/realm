Realm for iPhone is the companion to Realm, a free, open-source Mac app (https://realm.computer) that runs coding agents such as Claude Code and Codex. The iPhone app has no account, no sign-in and nothing to buy. It shows the work running in Realm on a Mac the person has paired it with, and lets them answer it from the phone.

Without a paired Mac, the app opens on Pair with your Mac: what Realm is, the three steps to pair, and a link to get Realm for Mac.

To see it work:
1. On a Mac with Apple silicon, download Realm from https://realm.computer and open it. Pairing needs no account.
2. In Realm, open Settings ▸ Devices and press Pair an iPhone. A QR code appears for five minutes.
3. In Realm for iPhone, tap Scan the code and point the camera at it, or tap Paste the link and paste the link shown under the code.
4. On the Mac, press Pair. The phone now lists the Mac's spaces and sessions.
5. A session runs an agent signed in on the Mac under the person's own account (Realm's first run offers Claude and ChatGPT sign-in). With one, start a session from the phone's compose button, answer its permission requests, and stop it.

How it reaches the Mac: through Realm's relay, a Cloudflare Worker. Everything between the phone and the Mac is encrypted end to end with keys made on the two devices, so the relay carries only ciphertext. A notification is encrypted on the Mac for one phone and opened by the app's notification service extension; Apple's push service sees only a placeholder.

The app asks for the camera, to scan the pairing code and to take photos to send to a session, and for notifications, after pairing.

Encryption: the app uses only Apple's CryptoKit (Curve25519, AES-GCM, SHA-256) and TLS, and declares ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption NO.
